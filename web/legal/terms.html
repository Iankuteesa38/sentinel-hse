Last updated: 12 August 2026

1. Purpose

Sentinel HSE is a workplace health, safety and environment management tool intended to assist users with hazard reporting, inspections, corrective actions, investigations, risk assessments, toolbox talks and related HSE activities.

2. Professional Responsibility

Sentinel HSE supports HSE activities but does not replace competent professional judgment, applicable laws, company procedures, permits, engineering controls, emergency requirements or regulatory obligations.

3. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for:

  • Providing accurate and appropriate workplace information.
  • Protecting their account credentials.

  • Following applicable workplace and company HSE requirements.

4. AI-Generated Content

Where Sentinel HSE provides AI-assisted analysis or recommendations, such outputs are intended as decision-support information and must be reviewed by a competent person before being relied upon for safety-critical decisions.

Records created within an organization's workspace may be accessible to authorized organization members according to assigned roles and permissions.
