Privacy Policy for Sentinel HSE

Effective Date: 14 August 2026

Sentinel HSE is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when using the Sentinel HSE application.

1. Information We Collect

Sentinel HSE may collect information entered or uploaded by users while using the application, including:

  • Inspection and audit information
  • Health, Safety and Environment records
  • Hazard and observation reports
  • Corrective and preventive action records
  • Incident investigation information
  • Vehicle and workplace inspection information
  • Photos uploaded or captured through the application
  • User-entered names, locations, companies, contractors, and project information

2. Camera and Photo Access

Sentinel HSE may request access to the device camera or photo library so users can attach photographs to inspections, incidents, hazards, observations, and other HSE records.

Camera and photo access is only used when the user chooses to capture or upload an image.

3. How Information Is Used

Information collected through Sentinel HSE may be used to:

  • Record and manage HSE inspections and audits
  • Generate inspection and investigation reports
  • Track hazards and corrective actions
  • Maintain workplace safety records
  • Improve application functionality and user experience

4. Data Sharing

Sentinel HSE does not sell users' personal information.

Information may only be shared when required for the operation of the application, requested by the user, required by an organization using the application, or required by applicable law.

5. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information stored or processed through Sentinel HSE. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality or to meet workplace, contractual, regulatory, or legal requirements.

7. User Control

Users may control application permissions such as camera and photo access through their device settings.

8. Children's Privacy

Sentinel HSE is designed for professional workplace Health, Safety and Environment activities and is not intended for use by children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as Sentinel HSE develops and new features are introduced. Updates will be published on this page.

10. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy or Sentinel HSE privacy practices, please contact the Sentinel HSE application administrator.
