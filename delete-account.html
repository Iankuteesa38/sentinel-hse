Sentinel HSE Account Deletion Request

Sentinel HSE users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Sentinel HSE account, please contact the Sentinel HSE administrator using the email address below.

Email: iankuteesa@gmail.com

Please use the subject: Sentinel HSE Account Deletion Request

In your request, include the email address or username associated with your Sentinel HSE account so that your account can be identified.

What Will Be Deleted

Upon verification of the request, Sentinel HSE may delete the user's account and personal information associated with that account, including account profile information and user-generated records where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained where required for legal, regulatory, contractual, security, fraud-prevention, workplace safety, audit, or compliance purposes.

Any retained information will only be kept for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will normally be reviewed and processed within a reasonable period after the user's identity and account details have been verified.
