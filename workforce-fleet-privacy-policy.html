Privacy Policy for Sentinel Workforce & Fleet

Effective Date: 14 August 2026

Sentinel Workforce & Fleet is committed to protecting the privacy of its users. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, and protected when using the Sentinel Workforce & Fleet application.

1. Information We Collect

The application may collect information entered or uploaded by users, including:

  • User names and email addresses
  • User account identifiers
  • Workforce and employee-related records
  • Attendance, duty, assignment, and operational information
  • Fleet and vehicle information
  • Driver and journey-related records
  • Inspection and maintenance records
  • Photos and documents uploaded by users
  • Company, contractor, project, and work location information entered by users

2. Camera, Photos and Documents

Sentinel Workforce & Fleet may request access to the device camera, photo library, or file storage so users can attach photographs or documents to workforce, fleet, inspection, maintenance, or operational records.

These permissions are used only when the user chooses to capture or upload content.

3. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Manage workforce and employee records
  • Manage fleet and vehicle information
  • Track drivers, assignments, and operational activities
  • Record inspections, maintenance, and compliance information
  • Generate operational and management reports
  • Support account authentication and application security
  • Improve application functionality and user experience

4. Data Sharing

Sentinel Workforce & Fleet does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared only when required for application operation, requested by the user or organization using the application, required for service delivery, or required by applicable law.

5. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information stored or processed through the application. Data transmitted between the application and supported online services is intended to use secure connections.

6. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide application functionality or to meet workplace, contractual, regulatory, audit, security, or legal requirements.

7. Account and Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data, subject to applicable legal, regulatory, contractual, audit, security, or operational retention requirements.

8. User Control

Users may control permissions such as camera, photo, and file access through their device settings.

9. Children's Privacy

Sentinel Workforce & Fleet is designed for professional workplace and operational use and is not intended for use by children.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time as the application develops or new features are introduced. Updates will be published on this page.
